ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Roads Safer with AI

Problem Statement

Driver fatigue and drowsiness are serious safety concerns that can lead to delayed reactions, loss of vehicle control, and road accidents.

Our Solution

This project uses computer vision and facial landmark detection to monitor the driver's face and identify signs of fatigue such as prolonged eye closure, yawning and abnormal head movements.

🧠

Intelligent Monitoring

The system continuously analyzes facial landmarks and identifies possible signs of driver fatigue.

HOW IT WORKS

From Camera to Alert

01

Camera Input

The driver's face is captured through a camera.

02

Face Detection

MediaPipe identifies facial landmarks in real time.

03

Analysis

Eye, mouth and head movements are analyzed.

04

Alert

The system generates an alert when fatigue is detected.

KEY FEATURES

What Our System Detects

👁️

Eye Closure Detection

Uses Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) to identify prolonged eye closure.

🥱

Yawn Detection

Uses Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) to identify possible yawning.

↕️

Head Nod Detection

Detects forward head movement that may indicate fatigue.

⚠️

Critical Alert

Escalates the warning when prolonged eye closure indicates a high accident risk.
